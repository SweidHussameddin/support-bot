# Nordlicht Haustechnik – Service conditions

Valid from 1 January 2026. These conditions apply to all maintenance, repair and emergency work carried out by Nordlicht Haustechnik.

## Appointments

Standard appointments are available Monday to Friday between 8:00 and 17:00 and are booked through the office. We confirm every appointment in writing with a two-hour arrival window. If a technician is running more than 30 minutes late, the office calls the customer.

Appointments can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours in advance. Later cancellations are charged at one hour of the standard labour rate.

## Emergency service

Weekend emergency service runs on Saturdays from 8:00 to 16:00 at the standard rate plus a 25 % surcharge. On Sundays and public holidays there is no regular emergency service. For a total heating failure in winter (outdoor temperature below 0 °C) we offer a Sunday callout at the standard rate plus a 50 % surcharge, subject to technician availability.

Emergency service covers heating failures, burst pipes and water leaks. It does not cover new installations, bathroom work or routine maintenance.

## Labour rates and travel

The standard labour rate is 78 EUR per hour, billed in 15-minute steps. Travel is charged as a flat 39 EUR within 40 km of our workshop and 0.95 EUR per kilometre beyond that.

## Warranty

Repairs carry a 12-month warranty on labour. Spare parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, usually 24 months. The warranty does not cover damage caused by frost, incorrect operation or work by third parties.

## Payment

Invoices are due within 14 days. Private customers can pay by bank transfer or card on site. We do not accept cash above 500 EUR.
