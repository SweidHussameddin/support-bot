# Nordlicht Haustechnik – Frequently asked questions

## Do you install heat pumps in older houses?

Yes. Most houses built after 1980 with radiators can be fitted with an air-to-water heat pump if the radiators are large enough for a flow temperature of 55 °C or lower. We check this during the free site visit and tell you honestly if it will not work.

## How long does a heat pump installation take?

Typically two to three working days for an air-to-water unit replacing a gas boiler. Ground source systems take longer because of the drilling, usually one to two weeks including the drilling contractor.

## Is there a government subsidy?

In 2026 the federal subsidy covers up to 70 % of eligible costs for replacing a fossil boiler with a heat pump, depending on income and the age of the old system. We prepare the application documents as part of the installation package. We cannot guarantee approval; the decision is made by the funding agency.

## What should I do if my heating fails?

First check the power supply and the pressure gauge on the boiler (it should be between 1 and 2 bar). If the pressure is low, top up according to the manual. If the system still does not start, call us. On weekdays we send a technician the same or next day; on Saturdays the emergency service applies.

## How often should a heat pump be serviced?

Once a year. Manufacturers require annual maintenance for the warranty to remain valid. Our maintenance package includes the written report you need for warranty claims.

## Do you work outside your region?

We work within about 80 km of our workshop. Beyond 40 km, travel is charged per kilometre. For larger projects further away please ask; we sometimes make exceptions.

## Can I get a quote by email?

For maintenance and small repairs we quote fixed prices from the price list. For installations and renovations we need a site visit first. Quotes are valid for 30 days.
