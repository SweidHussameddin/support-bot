# Nordlicht Haustechnik – Opening hours and contact

## Office and workshop

Monday to Thursday: 7:30 to 17:00
Friday: 7:30 to 15:00
Saturday: emergency service only, 8:00 to 16:00 (see service conditions)
Sunday and public holidays: closed

The office is closed between Christmas and New Year. Emergency service for heating failures runs during that period on weekdays from 9:00 to 15:00.

## Showroom

Our bathroom showroom is open Tuesday to Friday from 9:00 to 16:00 and on the first Saturday of each month from 9:00 to 13:00. Appointments are recommended for planning sessions.

## Reaching us

Customers reach the office by phone during office hours or through the contact form on the website. Written requests are answered within one working day. For emergencies outside office hours, the emergency number is printed on your maintenance report and on the sticker on your boiler.
